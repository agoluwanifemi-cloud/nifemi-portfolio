Why nowThe need

Nigeria has no shortage of Christianity. It has a shortage of grounding.

39%

The share of Nigeria's population projected to be Christian by 2050, down from roughly 43–46% today.

Pew Research Center
80–90%

Of pastors and church leaders across Africa have little or no formal biblical or theological training.

World Evangelical Alliance · Langham Partnership
Rising

Irreligion among young Nigerians, sharpest among men under 30, driven by disillusionment with a faith many were never truly taught.

World Values Survey, 2025

A faith inherited but not understood does not survive the questions of a new generation.

  • We serve, not compete. We equip a church's own people and leave the capability inside the church.01

A non-denominational ministry holding the historic Christian faith: the Bible as God's inspired and final Word; one God in three persons; Jesus fully God and fully man, crucified for our sins and raised bodily; salvation by grace through faith in Christ alone; and a church called to share and defend the gospel with clarity, courage and love.

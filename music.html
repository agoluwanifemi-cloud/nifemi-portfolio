In You

MANNORR · Single · Coming soon

The story

He gave Himself away, so I could live in Him.

I received this song in 2019, while preparing for the Philly concert.

After almost seven years of carrying this song and almost four years of production, I am happy to announce that I am ready to share this masterpiece with you. I pray it blesses you as much as it blessed me. 🔥🔥🔥

The recording was produced by Yimika DAkinson and Steph Ade.

IN YOU, COMING SOON…

In Him we live and move and have our being.

The Son of God, who loved me and gave Himself for me.

If the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed.

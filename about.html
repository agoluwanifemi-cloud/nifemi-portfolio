About

Visual and brand designer, preacher and songwriter. Lagos and Dublin.

Story

I make things that point past themselves.

I'm Nifemi Adeniran, known as MANNORR. For more than eight years I've designed brand identities, campaigns and visual systems for businesses, churches and creators.

I hold an MSc in Interactive Digital Media from Griffith College Dublin. I founded Apologetics Nigeria to help believers defend the faith and share the gospel, and I'm starting The Viral Church.

In January 2027 I release In You, a song about living in Christ. Design, music and teaching are different crafts serving the same calling.

What I believe

The purpose of every man is to know God and to make Him known.

Knowing Him requires diligence. Making Him known requires excellence, in every expression: a brand, a song, a sermon.
